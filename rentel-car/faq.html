Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How can I book a car?
You can book a car through our website by selecting your preferred vehicle, choosing the dates, and completing the payment process.
2. What documents are required for booking?
A valid driving license and a government-issued ID are required at the time of pickup.
3. Can I cancel my booking?
Yes, you can cancel your booking as per our cancellation policy. Refund eligibility depends on the cancellation time.
4. Is insurance included in the rental?
Basic insurance coverage is included. Additional coverage options may be available at extra cost.
5. What happens if the car breaks down?
In case of a breakdown, contact our support team immediately. We will provide assistance or a replacement vehicle if required.
