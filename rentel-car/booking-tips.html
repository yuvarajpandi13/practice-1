Car Booking Tips

Make your car booking experience smooth and hassle-free by following these helpful tips:

1. Book Early

  • Reserve your vehicle in advance to get better rates.
  • Early booking ensures vehicle availability during peak seasons.

2. Compare Prices

  • Check different car categories before confirming your booking.
  • Look for discounts, promo codes, or seasonal offers.

3. Check Rental Policies

  • Read cancellation and refund policies carefully.
  • Understand fuel policy, mileage limits, and late return charges.

4. Verify Documents

  • Carry a valid driving license and government-issued ID.
  • Ensure your license is valid for the rental location.

5. Inspect the Vehicle

  • Check the vehicle for any existing damage before driving.
  • Take photos as proof to avoid disputes later.

6. Understand Insurance Coverage

  • Confirm what insurance is included in your booking.
  • Consider additional coverage for extra protection.
Pro Tip: Always keep a copy of your booking confirmation and emergency contact number while traveling.

Following these tips will help you enjoy a safe, convenient, and stress-free car rental experience.
